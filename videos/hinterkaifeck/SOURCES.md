# Sources and evidence decisions

Research accessed 25 October 2026. The narration is an original English synthesis. Images are newly generated illustrative reconstructions; they are not crime-scene photographs, victim likenesses, evidence scans, or an exact architectural survey. No source photographs or article text are reused as visual assets.

## Sources actually read

1. [Hinterkaifeck.net — victims](https://www.hinterkaifeck.net/weitere-informationen/die-opfer/): names, birth dates, family relationships, Maria's arrival. A specialist community research site, not an official police publication.
2. [Hinterkaifeck.net — detailed chronology](https://www.hinterkaifeck.net/weitere-informationen/chronologie/): timeline; named witnesses; distinction between contemporary and late statements; uncertainty concerning animals; mechanic's account; discovery; demolition. Its own annotations identify hearsay and conflicting versions.
3. [Hinterkaifeck.net — location and scene](https://www.hinterkaifeck.net/weitere-informationen/der-tatort/): nearby village, L-shaped farm, connected rooms, discovery disturbance, remaining valuables; includes quoted historical statements. Graphic details were deliberately excluded from the film.
4. [Hinterkaifeck.net — preceding incidents](https://www.hinterkaifeck.net/basiswissen/gab-es-wirklich-schon-vor-der-tat-komische-vorfaelle/): reported damaged lock, newspaper, noises, former servant. Read alongside the more cautious chronology.
5. [Hinterkaifeck.net — weapon](https://www.hinterkaifeck.net/basiswissen/was-war-die-tatwaffe/): concealed Reuthaue, distinctive screw, quoted October 1923 prosecutor's report. The page has an apparent year typo in one sentence; the chronology independently within this site places demolition in February/March 1923. The narration uses the following year and does not repeat the typo.
6. [Hinterkaifeck.net — Karl Gabriel](https://www.hinterkaifeck.net/basiswissen/fiel-karl-gabriel-im-krieg/): wartime death, burial confirmation, comrades' accounts. Grounds for treating the secretly returning husband theory as unsupported.
7. [Hinterkaifeck.net — DNA limitations](https://www.hinterkaifeck.net/basiswissen/koennte-eine-dna-analyse-heute-noch-den-fall-klaeren/): distinction between answering kinship questions and proving responsibility. The film does not repeat the site's categorical prediction that no future analysis could help.
8. [Hinterkaifeck.net — archival holdings](https://www.hinterkaifeck.net/weitere-informationen/die-akten/): provenance and limitations of archival access. This production did **not** personally inspect original state archive files; linked summaries and selected quotations are not equivalent to a complete primary-source review.
9. Manuela Mayr, [Augsburger Allgemeine — Hinterkaifeck oder der Reiz des Ungewissen](https://www.augsburger-allgemeine.de/bayern/Kriminalgeschichte-Hinterkaifeck-oder-der-Reiz-des-Ungewissen-id19433916.html): regional reporting on research, lost scene evidence, 2007 student project, surviving uncertainty, and reported discovery of Karl Gabriel's grave. Full article text was accessible and read. The page's displayed publication/update labels are inconsistent; its narrative concerns the 90th anniversary in 2012.
10. Katie Serena, [All That's Interesting — The Gruesome True Story of the Unsolved Hinterkaifeck Murders](https://allthatsinteresting.com/hinterkaifeck-murders), published August 3, 2023; updated October 20, 2023: independent popular summary, basic timeline and suspects. Strong claims about the killer staying and the rejection of robbery were **not** adopted as facts.
11. Sonya Vatomsky, [Mental Floss — The Chilling Story of the Hinterkaifeck Killings](https://www.mentalfloss.com/article/502044/chilling-story-hinterkaifeck-killings-germanys-most-famous-unsolved-crime), July 24, 2023: additional popular retelling and comparison. Its uninjured-dog assertion conflicts with the specialist scene account; its exact war-death date and certainty about continued upkeep are not relied upon.

These are not eleven independent confirmations: eight entries belong to the same specialist research project, and popular articles share earlier sources. The script uses attribution and uncertainty rather than manufacturing independent corroboration.

## Exclusions and access limitations

No invented dialogue, narrated killer viewpoint, exact last meal, attack order, continuous four-day occupancy, supernatural explanation, or allegation against a living descendant. No claim that a private student analysis solved the crime. Wikipedia returned an access error during this research; it is not listed as a consulted source. A guessed official police URL and guessed Guardian URL returned 404 and were not used. Search snippets were used for discovery, not as evidentiary citations.
