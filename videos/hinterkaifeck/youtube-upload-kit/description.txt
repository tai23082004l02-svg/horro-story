In 1922, six people were killed at the Hinterkaifeck farm in Bavaria. Four days later, neighbors discovered the victims. A mechanic had worked there that same day without finding anyone to answer him.

Did somebody remain at the property after the murders? This documentary follows the timeline, the people, the physical clues, and the limits of the witness accounts. It separates documented history, reported observations, and later theories. No suspect is presented as a proven killer.

All visuals are AI-generated illustrative reconstructions, not genuine crime-scene photographs, authentic victim likenesses, or evidence scans. Narration is synthetic American English. The case involves the deaths of adults and children; no graphic violence is shown.
