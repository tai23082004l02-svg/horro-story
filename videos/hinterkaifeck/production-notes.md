# Production notes

- User authorized production after selecting the Hinterkaifeck topic. The earlier flat-color, black-outline 2D default was explicitly suspended; this film uses semi-realistic cinematic reconstruction illustrations with cold gray and amber lighting.
- English (US) original narration; Kokoro `am_michael`, speed 1.07. Name pronunciations use documented approximate phoneme overrides. No `atempo`, music, sound effects, shock stings or artificial duration padding. Ordinary short synthesis/section pauses remain.
- Final visual plan: 225 distinct selected image panels from fifteen retained 4×4 sheets, with straight cuts and approximately 4.09 seconds per shot. Fifteen panels in those retained sheets were rejected. A separate original discovery sheet was superseded in full before selection (sixteen additional unused panels). No recycled crop is counted as a new image.
- Delivery: 1280×720, 24 fps, H.264/yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz mono, English burned subtitles and separate SRT. Panel sources are about 418×235; resampling to 720p follows the accepted grid workflow and does not add source detail. No 1080p or sharpening trial.
- There are no on-frame chapter titles, top bars, counters, camera moves, fades or opaque subtitle bands. The standalone thumbnail contains text.
- Evidence discipline: no invented dialogue, attacker viewpoint, exact assault sequence or unqualified claim that the killer stayed four days. Suspects are discussed without a verdict. Original archives were not inspected directly; consulted summaries and their limitations are listed in `SOURCES.md`.
- The TTS script's phrase about Karl Gabriel was revised during production to refer clearly to the person responsible at Hinterkaifeck, then the entire affected section was resynthesized and caption/timeline offsets rebuilt. The delivered narration and transcript match.
- Trial renders exposed JPEG full-range metadata and captions frozen at still-image input timestamps. The final pipeline explicitly converts color range and generates 24 fps frames before the subtitle filter. Validation compares two frames within the same first shot to confirm a subtitle appears while the image stays static. Superseded trial renders are not delivered.
- The discovery sheet was revised to distinguish a neighbor from Andreas. Other rejected scenes included wrong person counts, a modern bicycle detail, a supposed post recipient inside the silent house, victim look-alikes after the deaths, and generated evidence portraits. Actual source selections are saved in the project.
- Audio is normalized in two passes with an initial true-peak ceiling of −2.5 dBTP to leave AAC headroom; the final encoded file is measured independently against approximately −16 LUFS and no more than −1.5 dBTP. Consult `validation-summary.json` for actual measurements, not predicted normalization output.
- The first encoded audio measured −16.77 LUFS / −1.37 dBTP and was not accepted. The retained raw narration was remastered with the saved two-pass filter plus a calibrated +0.85 dB pre-limiter trim and a transient limiter (`limit=0.6`, automatic makeup disabled, latency compensated). That intermediate AAC measured −16.94 LUFS / −3.24 dBTP; a final +0.95 dB trim was added and the audio freshly encoded from the retained raw narration. The existing video stream was copied unchanged. The measurements in `validation-summary.json` replace those rejected trials.
- Manual review scope: source grids and selected final video frames; no claim of full manual viewing or complete human listening. Caption coverage, decode, streams, timing, image uniqueness and final audio are checked automatically.
- No YouTube upload, schedule, comment or other channel action was performed. The upload kit contains suggested text only.
