Status as of 2026-10-25 (Asia/Saigon). Prepared by Muse from `MUSE-MASTER-PROMPT.txt`.

what failed QA, and what remains. No YouTube upload was made or scheduled.

## Completed and verified

- **Narration (complete, all 12 blocks / 595 words / 49 sentences).**
  One adult US-English documentary voice (Muse speech, default voice), speed 82
  for a natural pace after a first pass at speed 100 measured 182 wpm / 3:39 and
  was rejected as too brisk. Final pass measures **263.21 s total (4:23)**,
  ≈149 wpm of speech. File: `narration.mp3` (192 kbps); 48 kHz WAV master kept
  with the project (`audio/narration-full-s82.wav`).
- **Timed subtitles (complete).** `subtitles.srt` — 49 cues timed from the actual
  sentence audio files and measured pauses, not from word-count division.
  Max two lines per cue, following the script verbatim.
- **Timing map.** `timings.json` — per-sentence block, start, end and duration
  from the final narration; shot cutting must follow it.
- **Reference / design frame.** `images/reference-frame.png` (2048×1152):
  four-legged dark steel jacket, diagonal bracing, yellow deck, light-gray
  accommodation block at one end, green circular helideck on deck, process
  equipment at the other end, legs continuing as piles deep into sediment.
  Known simplification: bracing is drawn mainly above the waterline in this
  frame; clips must keep bracing along the full jacket. V1 frame rejected
  (legs ended in splayed feet on the seabed, piles barely visible).
- **Thumbnail (both versions).** `thumbnail-clean.jpg` and
  `thumbnail-text.jpg` (1280×720), from the reference frame; text version adds
  only “BUILT AT SEA?” in editing, per the brief.
- **Motion clips generated: shots 01–06** (each 10 s, 1248×704, H.264 + AAC,
  in `clips/shot-01.mp4` … `shot-06.mp4`), using the reference frame as the
  continuity input.
  - Shot 01 waterline opening — QA pass.
  - Shot 02 high aerial, no coastline — QA pass (helideck “H” is a standard
    marking, not garbled text).
  - Shot 03 heavy-lift vessel holding a deck module on taut rigging, calm
    weather, nobody under the load — QA pass.
  - Shot 04 complete platform / cutaway to seabed — QA pass as a schematic
    cutaway-style view.
  - Shot 05 schematic cutaway — **QA FAIL, must be regenerated.** The jacket
    came out yellow (design is dark steel) and the legs end in circular pads
    resting on the seabed instead of piles embedded in sediment.
  - Shot 06 schematic side view, piles extending below the seabed — QA pass.
- **Sequence 01 preview (captioned).** `preview-sequence-01.mp4`:
  1280×720, 24 fps, H.264 + AAC 48 kHz, 20.79 s. Shots 01–04 cut to the actual
  sentence timings of narration block 01; shot 05 is a **placeholder still of
