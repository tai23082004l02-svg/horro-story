## Editorial and illustrative decisions

- Main platform is a generic four-legged fixed jacket. Its colors, deck layout, cabins, crew and installation views are illustrative design choices, not claims about a named asset.
- The separate semi-submersible uses a buoyant hull and seabed moorings, consistent with the BSEE development-system descriptions. Do not show rigid legs continuing down to the seabed.
- General transport securing, safe load separation, checks/commissioning and unobstructed routes provide explanatory context, not a detailed engineered installation or safety procedure.
- No masses, water depths, crew totals, prices, construction schedules, accident stories, record claims or guaranteed storm survivability are supplied.
- Pile dimensions, embedment, rigging and soil are schematic; no dimensions are suitable for real engineering use. No oil reservoir drawn as an underground lake.
- Thumbnail and explanatory underwater cutaways must not be represented as actual photography.
- User rejected the proposed universal character/stakes/ending formula. This documentary does not impose it and does not make a new permanent channel identity.
