# Fiction and asset provenance

**This is an original fictional horror story.** Daniel Reed, Lena, Blackpine Relay, the events, the station rules, and the supernatural mechanism are invented for entertainment. No assertion is made that the story happened, that these are real victims, or that it represents an actual communications facility.

The script was written for this project in this session. No real criminal case, news article, existing creepypasta, film dialogue, historical photo, or third-party character is used as a source. Familiar genre devices such as a radio double and an isolated night shift are used in an original narrative.

Narration is synthesized with Kokoro `am_michael`, speed 1.07; it is not a recording of a real witness. Ambient sound, radio treatment, and knocks are generated procedurally from deterministic noise and oscillators in the retained project code; no stock soundtrack or third-party SFX recording is used.
